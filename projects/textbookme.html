Overview

Project Overview

TextBookMe is a localized native Android peer-to-peer marketplace engineered specifically for higher education students across Canada. The platform provides university and college students with an affordable, safe channel to buy, sell, and trade pre-owned course textbooks, study guides, and academic supplies directly within their respective campus radius.

Product Context & Objectives

University students face exorbitant textbook costs alongside steep campus bookstore commission margins. TextBookMe eliminates middlemen by connecting buyers and sellers on the same campus, requiring a lightweight, location-aware Android application that operates reliably on a broad range of budget and flagship Android devices.

Image Compression & Upload Flow

Mobile listings require high-resolution cover photos for buyers to inspect condition, yet unconstrained camera uploads quickly saturate bandwidth.

Implemented a background compression pipeline that downsamples bitmap buffers, strips unnecessary EXIF metadata, and converts photos to efficient JPEGs before multipart upload, reducing payload sizes by over 80%.

Marketplace Discovery Flow

01 Student opens app → GPS detects campus vicinity or user selects institution
02 Search by course code, department, author, or 10/13-digit ISBN
03 Detailed book inspect view with photos, highlighted pages notes, price
04 Direct in-app communication established for safe on-campus pickup
Engineering Challenges

Key Challenges Solved

Challenge: Fragmented Android Device Memory Limits

College students utilize a wide spectrum of legacy Android versions and low-RAM devices where large image feeds frequently trigger `OutOfMemoryError` crashes.

Solution Implemented:

Tuned Glide bitmap pooling with `RGB_565` format decoding for thumbnail lists, paired with proactive memory trimming hooks during system memory pressure events.

Challenge: Flaky Network on Campus Wi-Fi

Students moving between lecture halls experience frequent access-point handoffs, resulting in aborted multipart uploads when publishing new textbook listings.

Solution Implemented:

Implemented an idempotent upload queue with exponential backoff retries, ensuring draft listings remain safely saved locally until successfully confirmed by the server.
