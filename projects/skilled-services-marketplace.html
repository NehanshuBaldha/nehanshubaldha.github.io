Overview

Project Overview

Skilled Services Marketplace is an on-demand, two-sided mobile platform engineered to connect residential and business customers with vetted, specialized trade workers (plumbers, electricians, HVAC technicians, general maintenance). The system handles dynamic dispatching, real-time geolocation tracking, multimedia work proof verification, and automated milestone escrow payments.

Product Context & Objectives

Traditional home and field service booking suffers from opaque pricing, unverified arrival times, and contentious payment disputes. The objective was to build a real-time, transparent mobile platform with synchronized bi-directional state between customer and worker apps, ensuring safety, verified job completion, and fraud-resistant transactions.

User Journey

The Complete Marketplace Lifecycle

Visualized end-to-end execution chain connecting both sides of the marketplace.

01
Customer Request: Customer browses trade categories, enters project scope, and triggers on-demand dispatch or scheduled booking.
02
Worker Acceptance & Navigation: Nearby verified workers receive broadcast alerts with job specifics; upon acceptance, turn-by-turn route navigation initiates.
03
Live GPS Tracking: Background location service broadcasts worker coordinates via WebSocket/Socket.IO onto customer's Google Map in real time.
04
On-Site Job Execution: Worker marks arrival, clocks job start, and accesses client notes and access guidelines.
05
Multimedia Verification Proof: Worker captures mandatory before/after photos and video proof through camera integrations.
06
Review & Escrow Release: Customer inspects work, submits rating/review, and triggers automated release of Stripe/PayPal escrow payment.
Engineering Focus

Proof Capture Pipeline

International Client Collaboration

Engineering Challenges

Key Challenges Solved

Challenge: Mobile Battery Drain from Continuous GPS

High-frequency GPS updates rapidly drain worker device batteries and risk being suspended by iOS and Android OS background process throttles.

Solution Implemented:

Implemented dynamic distance-filter intervals: location broadcasts adaptively scale between high-frequency (when moving >15 km/h) and idle low-power modes (when stationary).

Challenge: Real-Time Race Conditions During Job Bidding

When multiple service providers attempt to accept the same job dispatch broadcast simultaneously, socket latency could create double-booking conflicts.

Solution Implemented:

Architected an optimistic UI state with server-acknowledged atomic locks, gracefully notifying subsequent bidders if the ticket was claimed milliseconds prior.
