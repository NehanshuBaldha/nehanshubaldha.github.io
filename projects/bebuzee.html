Overview

Project Overview

Bebuzee is a high-capacity mobile Super App designed to consolidate multiple digital services into a unified, fluid mobile ecosystem. The platform encompasses social networking, short-form video streaming (ShortBuz), real-time direct and group messaging, WebRTC-powered voice and video calls, e-commerce shopping, property listings, skilled on-demand services, creator monetization, and advertising.

Product Context & Objectives

When joining the engineering effort, the primary objective was to modernize an existing large-scale production application. This involved refactoring legacy architectural bottlenecks, stabilizing authentication flows, engineering the ShortBuz short-form video feature, and integrating reliable multi-gateway payment infrastructure for global commercial transactions.

Deep Dive

Technical Architecture & Implementation

Architected for modularity to support dozens of interconnected sub-services without cross-module regressions.

State Management & Data Pipeline

Adopted GetX for reactive dependency injection and targeted widget rebuilds, preventing expensive full-tree repaints during high-frequency events like video playback and real-time chat updates.

The data layer uses structured repository patterns that isolate RESTful API communication, token refresh interceptors, and local caching from presentation UI.

Engineering Problem Solving

Challenges & Solutions

Challenge: Video Feed Memory Bloat

In short-form vertical video feeds, loading consecutive high-resolution video players rapidly exhausts GPU and RAM budgets on mid-range Android devices, causing out-of-memory crashes and dropped frames.

Solution Implemented:

Implemented a 3-player recycling pool that disposes of players beyond a 1-item window, coupled with proactive chunk preloading and native texture surface caching.

Challenge: Cross-Platform Payment Consistency

Integrating multiple distinct payment SDKs (Stripe, PayPal, Apple Pay) across both iOS and Android created fragmented checkout states and conditional failure modes.

Solution Implemented:

Engineered an abstracted Payment Gateway Service with unified status callbacks, idempotent webhook confirmations, and localized retry routines.
